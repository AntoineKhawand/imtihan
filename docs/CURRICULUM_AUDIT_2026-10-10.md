# Curriculum audit — 10 October 2026

Scope: Bac Libanais EB9, Seconde, Première S and IB mathematics, physics and chemistry. This is a coverage audit, not certification that every objective is currently examinable. Existing saved chapter IDs are preserved.

## Bac Libanais

| Level | Current chapter counts (math / physics / chemistry) | Finding and next action |
|---|---|---|
| EB9 | 4 / 2 / 2 | Several official curriculum domains are absent. Corrected statistics to discrete-series mean and weighted mean; the Grade 9 matrix does not specify median. Check the current suspended-content circular before expanding examinable coverage. |
| Seconde | 2 / 1 / 1 | Functions/vectors, optics and solutions are a narrow selection. No complete grade-specific source reconciliation exists; do not call this a complete syllabus. |
| Première S | 3 / 2 / 1 | Chemistry is reduced to kinetics, while math/physics also cover only selected domains. Grade placement and current reductions need official secondary subject documents before additions. |

The [CRDP mathematics matrix](https://www.crdp.org/fr_test_crd/curriculum-content_details/le-curriculum-des-math%C3%A9matiques/anglais) places real-number/radical computation, polynomials, circle geometry, similarity, vectors and weighted mean at Grade 9. The [CRDP science matrix](https://crdp.org/en_test_crd/curriculum-content_details/curriculum-sciences/french) identifies additional domains including force equilibrium, springs, pressure, buoyancy, electrical energy and heat, and chemistry beyond atoms and balancing. These are candidate omissions, not automatic approval to add every historical topic to this year's exams.

Use CRDP's [suspended-content guidance](https://www.crdp.org/curriculum/explanatory-guide/project-pdf) alongside the base curriculum. The [official book directory](https://www.crdp.org/books-pdf) alone does not establish subject coverage: one downloaded second-secondary specimen proved to be **Technology**, not an MVP science textbook, and was excluded. Secondary validation remains open rather than being inferred from the French Bac programme.

## IB

| Offering | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| MYP5 | Two math chapters, one physics chapter, no chemistry chapters. | Incomplete product coverage. School-specific unit plans are needed; do not invent a universal fixed MYP chapter sequence. |
| AA SL | Five broad topic groups, with sparse objectives. | Topic groups present; detailed objective and assessment mapping remains open. |
| AA HL | Only algebra, calculus and vectors; omitted shared SL functions, geometry/trigonometry and statistics. | Fixed: retain existing HL IDs, include the shared SL core in algebra/calculus and add the three missing topic groups with HL IDs. |
| Physics SL/HL | A single combined level, five broad chapters; missing explicit greenhouse/fields coverage and SL/AHL boundaries. | Coverage and level-separation backlog; no claim that old nuclear/particle objectives certify the current guide. |
| Chemistry SL/HL | A single combined level, six chapters; missing explicit bonding/materials, periodicity, equilibrium and redox groups. | Coverage and level-separation backlog; kinetics is labelled HL despite an SL component in the current course. |

The [official AA guide](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-guide-en.pdf) requires HL students to study both SL and additional HL content across five topics. The [IB mathematics page](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/mathematics/) distinguishes the present course (final assessment November 2028) from the course taught from 2027, assessed from 2029. This change uses the present course, not the future syllabus.

For science reconciliation use the current IB [physics guide](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/physics-guide.pdf), [physics syllabus report](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/research/pdfs/physics-subject-report.pdf) and [chemistry syllabus report](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/research/pdfs/chemistry-subject-report.pdf). Indexed official topic outlines support the gaps above; direct guide downloads returned HTTP 403 in this environment. Complete subtopic/SL/AHL mappings are therefore still required before claiming full coverage.

## Acceptance criteria for the remaining work

Record source edition, assessment year, grade/track and any suspended topics for each objective. Keep saved IDs stable. Split SL/AHL in the data model and generation constraints before offering level-specific science completeness. Validate a chapter selection for every MVP level, then have a subject teacher review exam content; passing structural tests alone cannot certify a syllabus.
