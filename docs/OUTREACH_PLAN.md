**Contact:** `info@teacherssyndicatlb.com`, +961 1 614666 / +961 1 616376.
**⚠️ Caveat:** the syndicate's own domain didn't resolve when checked — the contact info
above comes from a Yellow Pages listing and LBCI news coverage, not the syndicate's own
site. **Verify the email/phone are still current before sending anything.**
**Rationale:** the single highest-leverage channel by reach (a real union, not just a
page) — but also the one requiring the most care, since a union contact expects a
formal, credible pitch, not a marketing blast. Worth a real phone call to confirm the
contact is current before emailing.
**Source:** [Yellow Pages listing](https://www.yellowpages.com.lb/en/business/syndicates-professional-associations/teachers-syndicate), [LBCI coverage](https://www.lbcgroup.tv/news/lebanon-news/180698/head-of-the-private-schools-teachers-association-t/en)

### 4. Edarabia — Lebanon private school directory
**What it is:** Real directory listing ~80 Lebanese private schools, filterable by
curriculum (French, IB, American — directly matches Imtihan's supported curricula). Each
listing has its own "Inquire" contact path.
**Source:** [edarabia.com/schools/lebanon](https://edarabia.com/schools/lebanon)
**Rationale:** this is the most scalable, targeted channel — you can filter directly to
"IB schools in Lebanon" or "French curriculum schools in Lebanon" and reach out
school-by-school via their own listed inquiry channel, not a scraped personal email.
Slower (one school at a time) but highest relevance per contact.
**Status:** Not started (0 of ~80 schools contacted).
