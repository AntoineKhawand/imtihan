# Existing blog refresh — 12 October 2026

Read-only live audit: 38 articles (9 code-based, 29 database-backed). The wider SEO audit covered 50 public pages and found no missing-page failures or broken internal links. Many title-length warnings came from long headlines plus repeated branding. The content scorer is a structural checklist, not a ranking prediction; word count alone does not establish usefulness.

## Implemented in code

All article templates emit BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList data with real image references, canonical identity and the actual logo. Visible diagrams are teaching illustrations, not product statistics. Original publication dates are preserved; modification dates come from genuine edits. Missing dates are omitted rather than invented. The shared JSON-LD serializer escapes authored text so it cannot close a script element.

The nine static articles have distinct titles/descriptions and practical teacher prompts with topic-specific review advice. Titles match index cards and related links. The preparation article includes a labelled, sourced TALIS workload chart. Unsupported time-saving, compliance and blanket ECF claims were removed or qualified where reviewed. The chart is international context, not Lebanese data or evidence of Imtihan performance.

The database template receives schema, visual, related-link and date improvements after deployment, but original database titles/bodies are not overwritten. Database edit saves now recompute read time; sitemap modification dates reflect those saves.
